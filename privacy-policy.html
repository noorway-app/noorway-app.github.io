Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

NoorWay (“the app”) keeps your reading, your saved ayahs and your location on your phone. It has no accounts and no analytics, and the developer runs no server that receives your information. The app shows non-personalised ads from Google AdMob, which helps keep it free. This policy explains what the app uses, how the ads work, and the outside services your phone contacts.

Location

NoorWay asks for approximate location, never precise location, and only while you’re using the app. It never tracks your location in the background.

  • Why: prayer times and the Qibla direction depend on where you are. Both are calculated on your phone.
  • When: only when you tap Use my location or the refresh button in the Prayer tab. The app doesn’t check your location at any other time.
  • What’s kept: the coordinates and the city name are saved on your device, so prayer times and notifications keep working offline.
  • Android’s location services: your phone’s own location service (on most devices, Google Play services) finds your approximate position, and Android’s geocoder turns it into a city name. These are part of Android, not NoorWay, and may contact Google under Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Your choice: prayer times and the Qibla need a location. You can decline the permission and use the rest of the app (reading, recitation, progress) normally.

Ads

NoorWay shows ads from Google AdMob in three places: a small banner under short ayahs, a short video before you get a video reel, and a banner on the adhan page if NoorWay was already open with your phone unlocked when the adhan began. Ads are never shown on the lock screen.

  • Non-personalised: the app asks Google for non-personalised ads only, rated for general audiences. They depend on things like the app and your general area, not on a profile of you.
  • No advertising ID: the app blocks Android’s advertising ID and its ad-topics features.
  • What Google receives: when an ad is requested, shown or tapped, Google’s software in the app sends Google information such as your IP address (from which it can estimate your general area), device and app details, an app-specific device identifier, whether you viewed or tapped an ad, and diagnostic data. Google uses this to deliver ads, measure them, and prevent fraud and abuse, as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • What Google doesn’t receive from the app: the location you save for prayer times, what you read, your saved ayahs, your prayer checklist or your reading progress.
  • Your consent: where the law requires it, such as in the European Economic Area and the UK, Google’s consent form appears before any ads are requested. To change your choice later, open Settings → Help & credits → Privacy options.
  • Tapping an ad takes you to the advertiser’s site or app, where its own privacy policy applies.

All notifications are scheduled on your device, including the ongoing prayer time notification and reading-plan reminders. NoorWay doesn’t use push notifications and doesn’t register your device with any server.

When you choose to hear a translation, the text is read aloud by your phone’s own text-to-speech engine. NoorWay hands the text only to that engine on your device; some engines use online voices, under their provider’s own privacy policy.

What NoorWay does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No personalised advertising, and no use of the advertising ID.
  • No analytics or crash reporting. Google’s ads software, described under Ads, is the only part of the app that sends information to a company for advertising.
  • No selling or renting of your information.
  • No collection of your contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera.

Deleting your data

What the app stores stays on your device, so you’re in control of it. To delete it, open Android Settings → Apps → NoorWay → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app. There’s nothing to delete on the developer’s side, because nothing is stored there. Information Google receives through ads is handled under the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

NoorWay’s content is suitable for all ages, but the app isn’t directed at children under 13, because it shows ads. The developer doesn’t knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Ads are limited to those rated for general audiences.
